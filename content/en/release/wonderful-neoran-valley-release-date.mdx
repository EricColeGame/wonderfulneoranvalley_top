## What Arrives in 2028: The Game Itself

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a narrative-driven roguelite monster collector. Combat revolves around the Timeline, a tool born from the Knight's foresight that reveals enemy Neoran actions several turns in advance. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase, spending Neura charges that build turn after turn. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris, while Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions and Traits apply passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.

Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield rather than a capture device, and Neoran evolve. The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals adding more.
