## Is Wonderful Neoran Valley coming to Switch 2?

Yes. Nintendo Switch 2 is one of the confirmed platforms for **Wonderful Neoran Valley**, alongside PC via Steam and PlayStation 5. The port was originally pitched as a Kickstarter stretch goal — "We're going console mode!" — and the campaign's stretch goals were unlocked, so the console version is no longer a hypothetical.

What does not exist yet is a firm launch date. Nara Studio is targeting a 2028 release window across the listed platforms, and no exact day has been announced. There is also no confirmed price, preorder bonus, or system requirement list for any version.

For first-party updates, the official site is [wonderfulneoranvalley.com](https://wonderfulneoranvalley.com/).

## How the Switch 2 port fits the release plan

Wonderful Neoran Valley began as a PC-focused crowdfunding project. The Switch 2 port and a PS5 port were both stretch goals, meaning console support depends on campaign performance rather than a separate publishing deal announced later.

Because the goals were reached, the game is now described as shipping on three platform families rather than one. That said, "confirmed platform" and "confirmed date" are different things. The 2028 window is a target, not a locked schedule, and Nara Studio has not broken it down platform by platform.

Treat any specific Switch 2 date, price, or eShop listing you see elsewhere as unverified unless Nara Studio or the official channels state it.

## What the Switch 2 version will actually play like

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a narrative-driven roguelite monster collector. You play a Knight of the Knights' Order, a rune-etched human who can absorb Neura, and you explore a valley split between human feudal cities and harsher Neoran biomes.

Recruitment is the defining twist: you do not catch Neoran with a device. You convince them through dialogue when you meet them on the battlefield, and each species reacts differently. Mehtyr, for example, argues with everything; Yakid is a durable coastal clan creature devoted to its clan's Wakiza.

Combat is built around the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase using Neura charges that build over turns. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris, Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, and Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.

The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with stretch goals adding more. The Knight is voiced by Troy Baker and Iris by Ben Starr, following the unlocked voiceover goal.
