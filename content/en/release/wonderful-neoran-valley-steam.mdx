Wonderful Neoran Valley has a live Steam store page, but that does not mean the game is out. Nara Studio’s narrative-driven roguelite monster collector is available to wishlist on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5473450/Wonderful_Neoran_Valley/), with a confirmed 2028 release window and no exact launch date yet. The official newsroom announced the Steam page on October 27, 2026.

## What the Wonderful Neoran Valley Steam Page Confirms

The Steam listing is official and currently serves as a wishlist page. The announcement asks readers to visit the store page, learn more about the game, and add it to a wishlist for future updates. It does not include a price, preorder offer, or system requirements.

As of October 29, 2026, no public playable demo has been announced. Those details are not included in the supplied official material, so they should be treated as unconfirmed rather than assumed.

## What Kind of Game Is Coming to Steam?

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a roguelite monster collector with a narrative focus. Neoran are intelligent creatures, and many can speak. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield, not by throwing a capture device.

The Knight’s foresight powers the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase and uses Neura charges that build over turns. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris. Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions. Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.

## Characters, Roster Scope, and the Steam Jewel

The Knight is voiced by Troy Baker, and Iris is voiced by Ben Starr. The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals planned to add more. Neoran can evolve.

The official gameplay material also mentions a Steam Jewel, an in-game artifact that boosts Blue and Red attacks when placed next to each other in the Timeline. That item is separate from the Steam storefront and should not be confused with the wishlist page.
