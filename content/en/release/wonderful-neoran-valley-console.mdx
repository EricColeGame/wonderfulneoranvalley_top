## What console players can expect from the game

The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals adding more. Launch languages are English, French, Spanish, and Italian; more may be added later. The campaign’s voiceover stretch goal was unlocked, and the verified cast includes Troy Baker as the Knight and Ben Starr as Iris.

Combat rewards planning rather than brute force. The Timeline shows enemy intentions in advance, while Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase using Neura charges that build over turns. Team building also involves Jewels, Sigils, and Traits.

No console-specific features, performance targets, or cross-save support have been confirmed in the supplied material.
