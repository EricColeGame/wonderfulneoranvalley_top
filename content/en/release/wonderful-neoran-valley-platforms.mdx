Wonderful Neoran Valley, the monster-collector roguelite from Nara Studio, is confirmed for three platforms: PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2. The studio is targeting a 2028 release window. No exact launch date has been announced as of October 29, 2026, and no price, preorder, or system requirement information has been published.

Both console versions have a documented history. During the game's Kickstarter campaign, "Switch 2 Port" and "PS5 Port" appeared as stretch goals, meaning the console releases were tied to crowdfunding milestones rather than being planned from day one. Those goals were reached, and the current platform lineup reflects the outcome.

The Steam listing is live at the official store page, which is the most reliable place to watch for store-level updates such as pricing and system requirements once Nara Studio publishes them.

## Languages and Audio at Launch

Launch languages are English, French, Spanish, and Italian. Nara Studio has said more languages may be added later, but nothing beyond those four is confirmed.

French and Spanish localization were themselves stretch goals during the campaign, which explains why the initial language list looks the way it does.

Voiceover was also a stretch goal and was unlocked. The confirmed cast includes Troy Baker as the Knight and Ben Starr as Iris. Whether voiceover ships in all four languages, or only some of them, has not been detailed publicly.

## What Carries Across Every Platform

No platform-exclusive Neoran, modes, or content has been announced. As far as Nara Studio has stated, the game is the same design on PC, PlayStation 5, and Switch 2.

That design is built around dialogue-based recruitment rather than a capture loop. Players talk Neoran into joining on the battlefield, and each species reacts differently — a Mehtyr, for example, responds to a firm counterargument rather than flattery.

Combat leans on the Knight's foresight and the Timeline, which previews enemy actions several turns ahead. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase using Neura charges that build over turns. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris, Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, and Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects. The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals adding more.
