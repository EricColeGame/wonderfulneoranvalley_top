The latest Wonderful Neoran Valley trailer was announced on October 27, 2026, alongside the game's Steam page. It is a reveal for Nara Studio's monster-collector roguelite, showing redesigned leads, new creatures, and the first look at gameplay and animated cutscenes. The official website offers a “Watch the trailer” link. No exact release date is attached; the confirmed window is sometime in 2028.

## What the Wonderful Neoran Valley Trailer Shows

The trailer introduces the redesigned Knight and Iris, the game's second lead character, and explores new Neorans. According to the official site, it also provides the first look at gameplay and animated cutscenes. Ben Starr and Troy Baker feature in the voice cast.

This is a reveal trailer rather than a full mechanics tutorial. It does not announce an exact release date, a public demo, or a complete roster. Those details remain outside the trailer's confirmed scope.

## Combat Revealed: Timeline, Synapse, and Neura Charges

Combat in Wonderful Neoran Valley revolves around the Timeline. The Knight's foresight makes it possible to see enemy Neoran actions several turns in advance, allowing players to plan offensive and defensive strategies.

The trailer shows a specific combat moment: a wild Biskem is about to use Cloudbreak with a +300% bonus damage. Instead of using a Neoran defensive action, the Knight uses Sagira's Silent Assassin at the cost of two Neura charges.

Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase, before the Timeline starts. Synapse actions consume Neura charges that build up turn after turn, so timing matters.

## The Knight, Iris, and the Voice Cast

The Knight is a soldier of the Order of Knights, genetically modified through Neura injections. The gift of foresight lets the Knight sense danger and hostile intent moments before they happen. After years of studying the Valley, the Knight is selected for a reconnaissance mission and seems to be searching for something. Troy Baker voices the Knight.

Iris is a baby dragon who has wandered the Valley for about eighty years. She can see the Neura Flux with the naked eye and condense it into magic jewels. She has sensed the Neura Clusters and intends to create the greatest Neura jewel of all time, though her ultimate goal is unclear. Ben Starr voices Iris.

## Jewels, Sigils, and New Neorans

The trailer shows the Steam Jewel, which boosts the power of Blue attacks and Red attacks when they are placed next to each other within the Timeline. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris.

Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, increasing their strength or altering how they work. They are less common than Jewels but more adaptable, according to the official site.

The trailer introduces new Neorans, but it does not list the full roster. The confirmed base scope is more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals adding more.
