## Design details the studio has confirmed

Recruitment is the detail most often misreported. Neoran are intelligent creatures, and many can speak. The Knight recruits them through dialogue on the battlefield rather than by throwing a capture device. There is no capture rate, weakened-state capture, or held-item taming loop to describe.

Combat centers on the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead and reflects the Knight's foresight. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase, spending Neura charges that build over turns. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris, Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, and Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.

One roster entry is confirmed: Yakid, a durable coastal clan creature that serves its clan's Wakiza.
