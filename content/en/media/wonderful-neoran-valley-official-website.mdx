## Characters and Neoran on the site

The Characters section currently profiles the game's two leads, while the Neoran section introduces the creatures they encounter.

### The Knight

A soldier of the Order of Knights, the crown's special forces, whose members are genetically modified through Neura injections. The Knight gained the gift of foresight, sensing danger and hostile intent moments before they happen. The profile notes a talent for planning fights, impulsiveness elsewhere, and a search for something unnamed. Troy Baker provides the voice.

### Iris

A baby dragon who has wandered the Valley for roughly eighty years, studying its customs far from the dragons' capital. Iris sees the Neura Flux with the naked eye and condenses it into magic jewels. She is described as a shrewd opportunist who has sensed the Neura Clusters and intends to forge the greatest Neura jewel of all time. Ben Starr provides the voice.

### Pero

The featured Neoran profile is Pero, a creature with an extremely underdeveloped brain that conveys thought only by spinning around. Peros mature on the branch where they are born, then fall to the ground as easy prey, placing them at the bottom of the valley food chain. The site notes they remain fearless because fear is too complex for them.

The Neoran section opens by explaining that Neoran are intelligent creatures, most of which can speak the human language, and that some can be dangerous. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield rather than through a capture device. The site's FAQ also confirms that Neoran evolve.

## Gameplay systems explained: Timeline, Synapse, Jewels, Sigils

The Gameplay section walks through the systems that define a run. The Gameplay section's structure is Fight, Try Again, Come Back Stronger — a framing that matches the roguelite loop.

**Timeline.** The Knight's foresight lets players see enemy Neoran actions several turns in advance. Order and synergy matter more than simply picking actions, and enemy Neoran often set up combos in their own portion of the Timeline.

**Synapse.** Iris binds the Neura flow in the Knight's arm to that of a Neoran on the field, letting party members act during the planning phase, before the Timeline begins. Synapse actions consume Neura charges that build turn after turn. Every Neoran has its own Synapse action with a unique effect and animation.

**Jewels.** Artifacts of Neura forged by Iris that passively grant team-wide bonuses. The site's example, the Steam Jewel, boosts Blue and Red attacks when placed next to each other in the Timeline.

**Sigils.** Modifiers applied to individual Neoran Actions that increase strength or change how an action works. The site describes them as rarer than Jewels but more adaptable.

Traits also appear in the Timeline description as passive modifiers, some with effects that apply to every creature on the field.
