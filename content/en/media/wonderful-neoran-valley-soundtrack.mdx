The **Wonderful Neoran Valley soundtrack** has started rolling out well ahead of the game itself. Nara Studio's narrative-driven roguelite monster collector is targeting a 2028 release on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, but four singles tied to the project are already streaming under the Wonderful Neoran Valley artist name.

This page covers only what is publicly verifiable: the tracks, their runtimes, their release year, and the playlist they sit in. Details about composers, a full album, and how each track is used in-game have not been confirmed.

## What has actually been released

The Spotify artist page for Wonderful Neoran Valley lists four tracks under "Popular" and marks each one as a 2026 single in the discography section. All four appear as standalone singles rather than as part of a numbered album.

## Cave Parade is a track, not a game mode

One title is worth flagging because it reads like gameplay. "Cave Parade" is a soundtrack single by Wonderful Neoran Valley — it is not a confirmed mode, location, boss, or event in the game.

Nothing in the available material describes what the track accompanies or where it plays. Treat the name as a song title until Nara Studio says otherwise. The same caution applies to "Shimmering Depths," "What's Brewing?," and "Facts and Logic": the titles are evocative, but no source connects them to specific Neoran, biomes, or battles.

## The official game soundtrack playlist

Alongside the singles, the artist profile carries an artist playlist titled "Wonderful Neoran Valley (Game Soundtrack)," credited to Wonderful Neoran Valley. The same title appears under the "Discovered on" section of the page.

The existence of that playlist is confirmed. What it contains beyond the four singles, whether it will grow, and whether it will eventually become a full OST release are all unconfirmed. No tracklist for a complete album has been published.

## Listener numbers are a snapshot, not a milestone

At the time this data was collected (October 28, 2026), the artist page showed 364 monthly listeners. Individual play counts displayed were roughly 7,900 for "Facts and Logic," 10,900 for "Cave Parade," 8,000 for "What's Brewing?," and 8,800 for "Shimmering Depths."

These figures move constantly and should be read as a single moment in time rather than a permanent statistic. They are also small enough that they say little about the project's eventual reach — the game is still years from launch.
