## Is There an Official Wonderful Neoran Valley Discord?

Yes. Nara Studio runs an official Discord server for Wonderful Neoran Valley, and it is open to everyone — you do not need to have backed the Kickstarter to join. Because it is the studio's own space, it is the most direct place to check for official announcements and discussion about the game.

Two things the available evidence does not tell us: how many members the server has, and what specific channels or roles exist inside it beyond the backer channel described below. Treat any precise member count or channel list you see elsewhere as unverified.

## What Backers Get: The Alpha/Beta "Feedback" Channel

The project FAQ answers one Discord question directly. A backer asked whether the Alpha/Beta "Feedback" channel is included in Limited Tier rewards such as "Superfan" or higher. Nara Studio's answer was yes — and access was extended to all Limited Tier backers "due to popular demand." That FAQ entry was last updated March 1, 2026.

Note the wording. This is a channel tied to alpha and beta feedback, not a general chat perk, and it sits inside the campaign's reward structure. Access logistics are handled as the campaign rolls out, so follow official updates rather than secondhand summaries.

## What the Community Is Talking About

The confirmed picture: Wonderful Neoran Valley is a narrative-driven roguelite monster collector from Nara Studio, targeting a 2028 release on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2. No exact date, price, or preorder details have been announced.

On the game side, the studio has promised more than 70 recruitable Neoran at base scope, with campaign stretch goals adding more. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield rather than a capture device, and Neoran are intelligent creatures — many can speak. Combat runs on the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead, while Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase using Neura charges.
