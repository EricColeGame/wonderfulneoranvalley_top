Wonderful Neoran Valley, the monster-collector roguelite from Nara Studio, ran a Kickstarter campaign that closed on 29 March 2026. According to the campaign’s closing update, it raised €910,453 from 13,956 backers. The [official Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/narastudio/wonderful-neoran-valley) organized rewards into digital-only tiers, tiers with physical goods, and premium co-design tiers. The available campaign material does not restate every tier price or item list, so this article sticks to the confirmed structure rather than guessing at package values.

## How the Kickstarter Rewards Were Organized

The campaign split rewards into three practical groups. Digital-only tiers carried no additional shipping costs unless a backer chose physical add-ons. Tiers with physical goods required shipping, as the campaign’s shipping paragraph explained. Premium Co-Design tiers were also digital-only, but backers could opt into non-limited physical goods from a previous tier—specifically the Physical Artbook and Celebratory Promo Card—by paying only the shipping cost. That structure let backers avoid physical costs if they wanted a digital package, while still allowing them to add select physical items.

## Digital-Only and Physical Reward Tiers

Digital-only tiers were aimed at backers who wanted the game and campaign extras without shipping. The campaign page notes that these tiers had no additional shipping costs unless a backer purchased physical add-ons. Physical tiers included goods that required shipping; the supplied material does not provide a single flat shipping rate. The named non-limited physical goods are the Physical Artbook and Celebratory Promo Card. The official FAQ also says Kickstarter perks include the official artbook and that merchandise ships closer to release, but it does not list a page count, ISBN, or separate retail edition.

## Premium Co-Design Rewards

Premium Co-Design tiers offered four one-hour meetings with members of the Nara Studio team. In the first meeting, the backer pitched an idea and defined its key design elements with the developers. The next two meetings covered gradual progress from designers and artists, with feedback collected along the way. In the fourth meeting, the backer became the first person outside the team to see the final version. Nara Studio’s designers retained final say on game balance and on whether a co-designed item was appropriate.

## Stretch Goals That Expanded the Campaign

The campaign page lists stretch goals that expanded the project beyond the base reward structure. The final voiceover stretch-goal description states that all stretch goals were unlocked.

### Modes and Challenge Options

Mode goals included Rarities, Challenge Mode, Randomizer Mode, Hard Mode, Endless Mode, Brawl Mode, and First Try Mode. Challenge Mode was pitched for Nuzlocke-style runs with restrictions such as no healing, no evolutions, and persistent negative status. Randomizer Mode was described as adjustable randomness for Neoran spawns, stats, and move pools. Hard Mode was framed as higher enemy levels and tougher recruitment. Endless Mode would continue a run until the player loses. Brawl Mode would focus on arena battles without exploration or quests.

### Neoran, NPCs, and Questlines

Content goals added new Neoran, new Neoran NPCs, and new questlines. The campaign page lists two separate “5 New Neoran” goals and a final independence goal that adds five more, for a total of 15 new Neoran available on release. The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran. New Neoran NPC goals added friendly faces and a merchant-type character. Questline goals promised more lore about the Neoran, their factions, and how they coexist.

### Platforms, Localization, and Voiceover

Platform and production goals added a Nintendo Switch 2 port, a PS5 port, French and Spanish localization, and professional voiceover. The verified €900,000 stretch goal was the PS5 port. The voiceover goal states that all stretch goals were unlocked. The confirmed cast includes Troy Baker as the Knight and Ben Starr as Iris. The game is targeting a 2028 release on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2. Launch languages are English, French, Spanish, and Italian, with more possibly added later.
