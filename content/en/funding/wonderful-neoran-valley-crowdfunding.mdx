Wonderful Neoran Valley is a monster-collector roguelite from Nara Studio in which you recruit creatures called Neoran through battlefield dialogue rather than capture devices. Its Kickstarter campaign, run through the [official Nara Studio project page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/narastudio/wonderful-neoran-valley), closed on 29 March 2026 with €910,453 pledged by 13,956 backers, according to the campaign's closing update. That result turned a modest indie pitch into a funded production with console ports, localization, and professional voiceover attached.

## Campaign results at a glance

The closing update is the most reliable snapshot of how the campaign finished. The live page may still display a late-pledge option and a different running total, so the figures below are the ones to cite for the campaign itself.

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign closed | 29 March 2026 |
| Total pledged | €910,453 |
| Backers | 13,956 |
| Reported milestones | 600% on 2 March, 700% on 8 March, 727% on 14 March |
| Named targets | €600,000 for artistic independence; €900,000 stretch goal for a PS5 port |

Those percentage milestones are the campaign's own reported figures and are listed as-is. They should not be read as multiples of the €600,000 independence target, and the reviewed updates do not restate an original base-goal amount.

## What the funding targets actually meant

The €600,000 figure was framed around artistic independence rather than as the campaign's original base goal. Hitting it meant Nara Studio could keep producing the game on its own terms. The €900,000 stretch goal was specifically the PlayStation 5 port, which places the final €910,453 total just past that threshold.

Because the reviewed updates never restate a starting goal amount, any specific "base goal" number circulating elsewhere should be treated as unconfirmed. What is confirmed is the pair of named targets, the closing total, and the backer count.

## The stretch goal ladder

The campaign published a long ladder of stretch goals, and the final voiceover goal's own description states that backers unlocked all of them. The list spans difficulty and replay options — Challenge Mode, Hard Mode, Endless Mode, Randomizer Mode, Brawl Mode, First Try Mode, and a Rarities tier — alongside new questlines, additional Neoran NPCs, and Limited Edition Collector merchandise.

Creature content scaled in blocks of five. Base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, and the Full Independence goal added five more for a total of 15 new Neoran available at release. The ladder also covered French and Spanish localization, the Switch 2 port, the PS5 port, and professional voiceover.

## Rewards: digital, physical, and co-design

Reward tiers split into digital-only and physical goods. Digital tiers carry no shipping costs unless a backer adds physical items, while tiers containing physical goods require shipping. Some digital tiers let backers opt into non-limited physical extras such as the artbook or promo card by paying shipping only.

The Premium Co-Design tiers are the most involved option. They include four one-hour meetings with the development team: a pitch and design session, two progress reviews with designers and artists, and a final reveal. Nara Studio retains the last word on balancing and on whether a co-designed item is appropriate for the game.

## After the campaign: platforms, languages, and 2028

Wonderful Neoran Valley targets a 2028 release window, with no exact date announced. Confirmed platforms are PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2. Launch languages are English, French, Spanish, and Italian, with more possible later. The voiceover stretch goal ties to a confirmed cast: Troy Baker voices the Knight and Ben Starr voices Iris.

No public playable demo has been announced as of 29 October 2026, and no price, preorder bonus, or system requirements have been published. Anyone quoting those details today is working ahead of the official information.
