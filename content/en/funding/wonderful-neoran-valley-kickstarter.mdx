The **Wonderful Neoran Valley kickstarter** campaign is over. According to the campaign's closing update, Nara Studio's monster-collector roguelite finished with **€910,453 pledged by 13,956 backers** on **29 March 2026**.

That result carried the project past every stretch goal listed on the campaign page, including console ports and professional voiceover. This article covers only what the campaign itself reported, and separates confirmed plans from figures that the updates never restated.

The €600,000 figure attached to the campaign concerned **artistic independence** — producing the game without seeking publisher funding. The reviewed updates do not restate the original base-goal amount, so it should not be described as €600,000.

## How the funding climbed

The campaign reported three percentage milestones as it progressed. It announced **600% funding on 2 March**, then **700% on 8 March**, and **727% on 14 March**. These percentages track momentum rather than new content, and the underlying base used for them is not restated in the reviewed material.

By the close, the total had reached €910,453 — enough to clear the €900,000 threshold attached to the PlayStation 5 port. The campaign page also lists a Nintendo Switch 2 port among its stretch goals, and its final voiceover section states plainly that backers unlocked all stretch goals.

What the campaign does not provide in the reviewed text is a per-tier price list or a shipping cost table. Those details appeared in the campaign's reward images and its "IMPORTANT" shipping paragraph, which are not reproduced here.

## Stretch goals and what they add

The campaign's stretch goal list reads like a content roadmap. Gameplay additions include **Rarities**, a **Challenge Mode** with restrictions such as no healing or no evolutions, a **Randomizer Mode**, **Hard Mode**, **Endless Mode**, **Brawl Mode**, and a permadeath-style **First Try Mode**.

Beyond modes, the list adds new **Neoran**, new **Neoran NPCs**, and extra **questlines**. Three separate "5 New Neoran" goals appear on the page; the full-independence goal describes them as reaching "a total of 15 new Neoran available on release."

Platform and production goals cover the **Switch 2 port**, the **PS5 port**, **FR/ESP localization**, **professional voiceover**, and a **Limited Edition Collector Pack** for the merch store, with Premium Reward Tier backers getting priority access.

## Reward tiers and shipping

Reward tiers split into digital-only and physical groups. Digital-only tiers carry no shipping charges unless a backer adds physical items. Tiers that include physical goods require additional shipping, as the campaign's shipping paragraph explains.

The **Premium Co-Design tiers** are the most involved option. They include four one-hour meetings with the developer team: a pitch and design session, two progress reviews with designers and artists, and a final reveal of the finished creation. Nara Studio retains final say on balancing and on whether a co-designed item is appropriate for the game.

Backers on digital-only tiers can opt into non-limited physical goods from earlier tiers — the Physical Artbook and Celebratory Promo Card — by paying only shipping.

## What the campaign confirmed about the game

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a monster-collector roguelite built around recruitment rather than capture. The campaign states directly that you cannot "catch" Neoran as in a typical monster collector; instead you recruit them through dialogue when you meet them on the battlefield.

The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, before the stretch goal additions. Confirmed roster names include Yakid, Ankhat, Reversilk, Valiant, Syllva, Noctivora, Sagira, Mehtyr, and Pero.

Combat leans on the Knight's foresight and the **Timeline**, which reveals enemy actions several turns ahead so players can plan efficient turns rather than react. The game targets a 2028 release window on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, with no exact date announced.
