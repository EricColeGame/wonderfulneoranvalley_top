Nara Studio's Kickstarter for Wonderful Neoran Valley closed on 29 March 2026 with **€910,453 pledged by 13,956 backers**. Those numbers come straight from the campaign's own closing update, which described the month as one that "changed our lives."

The funding question has two parts: the target the campaign started from, and the stretch goals layered on top of it. Nara Studio's updates report progress in percentages and euro thresholds rather than restating a base goal figure, so this article sticks to what the campaign record actually documents.

## The final Wonderful Neoran Valley funding numbers

| Item | Figure |

| Total pledged | €910,453 |

| Campaign closed | 29 March 2026 |
| Reported funding level | 600% (2 March), 700% (8 March), 727% (14 March) |

The base funding target itself is not spelled out in the campaign updates reviewed here. If you need the original goal amount as listed at launch, confirm it on the [official Kickstarter page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/narastudio/wonderful-neoran-valley) rather than relying on secondhand summaries.

What the updates do make clear is the pace. The project hit 600% funding on 2 March 2026 and 700% on 8 March, less than ten days after those milestones began stacking up.

## What each percentage milestone was tied to

Nara Studio framed the 600% mark as a creative independence threshold. Reaching it, the team wrote, meant they would not have to compromise on "some of the less conventional artistic elements of the game" when exploring outside funding opportunities. In other words, the money was presented as protection for the art direction, not as a scope increase.

By 14 March the campaign sat at 727% funding, with three percentage-based stretch goals still ahead at 800%, 900%, and 1,000%. The team did not publish a breakdown of what each of those three tiers contained in the updates available here, so their contents remain unconfirmed.

A separate, euro-denominated stretch goal was announced in the same mid-March window.

## The €600,000 and €900,000 stretch goals

Two stretch goals were described in hard currency rather than percentages. The **€600,000** target was the artistic compromise line referenced above. The **€900,000** target, revealed during the "Meet the Devs #2" stream on 14 March 2026, was a PlayStation 5 port.

Nara Studio attached a warning to that PS5 goal: the port would "most likely release AFTER both the Switch 2 and the PC versions." Backing the goal meant the game would eventually be playable on PS5, not that it would arrive there on launch day.

The campaign finished at €910,453, above that €900,000 threshold. PS5 appears among the confirmed platforms for the 2028 release window, alongside PC via Steam and Nintendo Switch 2.

## What extra funding changed — and what it didn't

More money did not mean a bigger game. When asked whether surplus budget would expand the project, the studio said it would look to expand the workforce instead: "The game will be the same, but better, and made by more people."

The confirmed base scope is already substantial: more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with campaign stretch goals adding further creatures to the roster. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield rather than a capture device, and combat runs on the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead.

Two things backers should not expect: no public playable demo has been announced, and no exact release date exists beyond the 2028 window.

## Pledge Manager timing and what backers are still waiting on

In May 2026, Nara Studio said surveys and the Pledge Manager would open "in the coming weeks." By the October 2026 update, that had changed. The studio explained that opening the Pledge Manager more than a year before shipping would force it to lock in shipping details far too early, risking problems at fulfillment.

The process was therefore postponed indefinitely, with updates promised as the next step approaches. Practical consequences for backers: shipping costs, address confirmation, and add-on management are still pending, and physical rewards are expected closer to release.
