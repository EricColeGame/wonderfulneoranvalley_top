Yakid is one of the creatures confirmed for the official Neoran roster in **Wonderful Neoran Valley**, the roguelite monster collector from Nara Studio. It is described as a durable coastal clan creature that serves its clan's Wakiza.

That description is the extent of what has been published so far. No stat block, action list, Synapse animation, or evolution path for Yakid has been shown. This article separates what the official material confirms from what remains unknown, and points to the channels where details will appear first.

## What Is Yakid in Wonderful Neoran Valley?

Yakid belongs to the Neoran — the intelligent creatures that populate the Valley. The official material notes that most Neoran can speak the human language and that some are dangerous, though the site is careful to say those definitions do not apply to every creature.

Within that wider group, Yakid stands out for two confirmed traits. It is **durable**, and it is tied to a **coastal clan**, where it serves that clan's Wakiza. The word Wakiza appears in the official description of Yakid without further explanation, so the exact role it describes inside Neoran society has not been detailed publicly.

What the evidence does not support is any claim about Yakid's combat numbers, rarity, elemental leaning, or growth. Those details are simply not published yet.

## Where Yakid Sits in the Wider Neoran Roster

Yakid is part of a large cast. The game promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran at its base scope, with campaign stretch goals adding more beyond that. The official site already introduces several named creatures — Pero, Sagira, and Biskem among them — and Yakid joins that confirmed list.

Neoran are not interchangeable monster slots. The site stresses that each recruited creature brings its own arsenal of Actions, and that Synapse gives every Neoran a unique action with its own effect and animation. If that design holds for Yakid, recruiting it would add a distinct way of manipulating time to a party — but the specific effect has not been revealed.

The official FAQ also confirms that Neoran evolve, without specifying which creatures have evolution lines or what those lines look like.

## How Recruitment Works — and Why It Matters for Yakid

Wonderful Neoran Valley does not use a capture device. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield, which fits the setting's framing of Neoran as intelligent, often speaking creatures rather than wild animals to be trapped.

That distinction shapes how players should think about Yakid. It is not a creature you weaken and then bag; it is a character you talk to during a fight. Whether Yakid is recruitable in every run, appears only in coastal encounters, or is tied to a specific story beat has not been confirmed.

Combat itself revolves around the Timeline, the interface built on the Knight's foresight, which reveals enemy Neoran actions several turns in advance. Order and synergy matter more than picking strong moves in isolation.

## The Systems That Will Define Yakid's Build

Even without Yakid-specific data, the published systems show how any Neoran gets customized during a run.

- **Jewels** are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris. They are described as the foundation of a solid strategy and the element that most defines a build.
- **Sigils** modify individual Neoran Actions, making them stronger or changing how they work. They are rarer than Jewels but more adaptable.
- **Traits** are passive modifiers, and some have effects that apply to every creature on the field.
- **Synapse** lets party members act during the planning phase, before the Timeline starts, at the cost of Neura charges that accumulate turn after turn.

Because Jewels are team-wide while Sigils attach to specific Actions, Yakid's usefulness will likely depend on how its eventual Actions pair with the rest of a party. That is inference from the system design, not a confirmed statement about Yakid.

## What Remains Unconfirmed About Yakid

Nara Studio has not published a Yakid stat line, signature move, Synapse effect, evolution, or recruitment condition. Anyone presenting those details today is extrapolating.

Equally unconfirmed: whether Yakid appears in the Lucca Comics & Games 2026 booth demo material, whether it is shown in the newest trailer, and how its coastal clan ties into the story of the Knight and Iris. The official channels are the place to watch for those reveals.

For now, Yakid's confirmed identity is narrow but clear — a durable coastal clan Neoran serving its Wakiza, part of a roster of more than 70 recruitable creatures. Details will fill in as development continues toward the 2028 window.
