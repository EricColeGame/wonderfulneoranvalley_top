## The Confirmed Leads: The Knight and Iris

The Knight is a soldier of the Order of Knights, the crown’s special forces. Its soldiers are genetically modified through injections of Neura, and the Knight gained the gift of foresight from that process. That power lets the Knight sense danger and hostile intent moments before they happen.

The official profile describes the Knight as skilled at planning fights, impulsive elsewhere, and driven by duty. It also says the Knight seems to be searching for something. Troy Baker voices the character.

Iris is a baby dragon who has wandered the Valley for about eighty years, studying its ways and customs. She can see the Neura Flux with the naked eye and condense it into magic jewels that influence its course.

The official profile calls Iris a shrewd opportunist who is willing to make immoral or controversial choices to reach a goal, including striking a deal with a human soldier. Ben Starr voices Iris. The Knight and Iris are the two lead characters, and their partnership is central to the game’s Synapse system.

## How Characters and Recruitment Fit the Roguelite

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a narrative-driven roguelite monster collector from Nara Studio. Its combat revolves around the Timeline, a tool that lets the Knight see enemy Neoran actions several turns ahead. Players plan around order and synergy, not just which actions are available.

Synapse extends the Knight’s time-altering ability to party members. Synapse actions can be used during the planning phase and consume Neura charges that build over turns. Jewels are team-wide passive artifacts forged by Iris. Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, while Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.

The official FAQ confirms that Neoran evolve. It also confirms a 2028 release window, with PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2 versions planned. Launch languages are English, French, Spanish, and Italian, with more possible later. No exact release date is confirmed.
