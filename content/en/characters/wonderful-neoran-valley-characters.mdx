The confirmed Wonderful Neoran Valley characters currently center on two leads: the Knight, a genetically modified soldier with foresight, and Iris, a baby dragon who can see and shape Neura. The [official website](https://wonderfulneoranvalley.com/) also profiles the Neoran Pero and names other creatures in gameplay examples, while the supplied material does not publish the full roster. Nara Studio’s roguelite monster collector is targeting a 2028 release on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2.

## Who Are the Confirmed Wonderful Neoran Valley Characters?

The official site separates its presentation into Characters and Neoran sections. That split is useful: the Knight and Iris are the story leads, while Pero is a creature profile. The named figures and creatures supported by the supplied material include:

- **The Knight** — voiced by Troy Baker. A soldier of the Order of Knights with Neura-granted foresight.
- **Iris** — voiced by Ben Starr. A baby dragon who sees Neura Flux and forges jewels.
- **Pero** — a Neoran whose only apparent way to communicate is spinning.
- **Yakid** — a confirmed Neoran in the official roster, described as a durable coastal clan creature that serves its clan’s Wakiza.

## The Knight: A Foresighted Soldier Voiced by Troy Baker

The Knight is the central soldier of the Order of Knights, the crown’s special forces. Its members are genetically modified through injections of Neura, and this Knight gained foresight: the ability to sense danger and hostile intent moments before they happen.

After years of studying the Valley through history and geography manuals, the Knight was selected for a reconnaissance mission. The profile notes skill at planning fights, impulsiveness elsewhere, an absolute sense of duty, and a search for something unspecified. Troy Baker voices the role. The Knight loves planning events in advance and hates Iris’s teleporting jewel.

## Iris: A Dragon Who Forges Neura Jewels

Iris is the game’s second lead: a baby dragon who has wandered the Valley for about eighty years, studying its customs far from the dragons’ capital. She can see the Neura Flux with the naked eye and condense it into magic jewels that influence its course.

The official profile calls her a shrewd opportunist willing to make immoral or controversial choices, including a deal with a human soldier. She has sensed Neura Clusters—buildup points in the Flux—and wants to create the greatest Neura jewel ever. Ben Starr voices her. She loves swaying on the Neura and has severe celiac disease.

## Pero and the Wider Neoran Roster

Neoran are generally intelligent creatures, and many can speak, but Pero is an exception. Its brain is extremely underdeveloped, and its only way to convey a thought is by spinning around. Peros live on the branches of the tree where they are born until maturity, then fall to the ground and become easy prey. That places Pero at the bottom of the food chain, yet it is fearless because fear is too complex for its tiny, smooth brain.

Beyond those profiles, official gameplay material names Sagira and Biskem. Sagira’s Silent Assassin is used in an example to stop a wild Biskem’s Cloudbreak; these are combat examples, not full biographies. Yakid is confirmed in the official Neoran roster. The game promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran at base scope, with campaign stretch goals adding more. Recruitment happens through dialogue on the battlefield, and Neoran can evolve.

## How Characters Shape Combat

Character design and combat are linked through the Knight’s foresight. The Timeline shows enemy Neoran actions several turns ahead, letting players plan order and synergy rather than only choosing actions.

Synapse binds the Knight’s Neura flow to a Neoran, letting party members act during the planning phase. Each Neoran has a unique Synapse action, and these consume Neura charges that build over turns. Iris forges Jewels, team-wide passive artifacts, while Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions and Traits add passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects. These systems explain why the roster matters mechanically, not just narratively.

## FAQ

### Are all Wonderful Neoran Valley characters confirmed?

No. The official site profiles The Knight, Iris, and Pero, and Yakid is confirmed in the official Neoran roster. Sagira and Biskem are named in gameplay examples, but the full roster and profiles have not been published in the supplied material. The base scope promises more than 70 recruitable Neoran, with stretch goals adding more.

### Who voices the two leads?

Troy Baker voices The Knight, and Ben Starr voices Iris. The official trailer announcement also names both actors.

### How are Neoran recruited?

Through dialogue on the battlefield, not by throwing a capture device. The supplied evidence does not describe capture rates, weakened-state capture, or type matchups, so those mechanics should not be assumed.

### Do Neoran evolve?

Yes. The official FAQ confirms that Neoran evolve. It does not provide evolution methods, levels, or forms in the supplied material.
