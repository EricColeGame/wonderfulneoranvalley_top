## The Knight and Iris: the confirmed human cast

You play as the Knight, the best recruit in the latest wave of cadets trained in the human kingdom. Knights are humans who accepted rune-etching to absorb Neura, making them unnatural hybrids. This Knight's power manifests as tactical foresight — the ability to read enemy intentions ahead of time.

The Knight is voiced by Troy Baker, and Iris is voiced by Ben Starr. Iris is tied to the game's Jewels, team-wide passive artifacts she forges. The campaign states that you can choose the Knight's gender, though the differences are described as purely aesthetic.

Neither character is a Neoran, which is why neither belongs in a creature roster list alongside Yakid or Sagira.

## How recruitment actually works

Wonderful Neoran Valley does not use a capture loop. You cannot throw a device at a weakened creature and add it to your party. Instead, you recruit Neoran through dialogue when you meet them on the battlefield.

Each species reacts differently to the Knight's presence in its habitat, and knowing a creature's desires and preferences is described as essential to winning it over. Mehtyr, for example, stays reticent unless you can match its objections, and loses interest if you fail to take a firm stand. Other Neoran react badly to tough love and may read it as hostility.

Combat supports this with the Timeline, which shows enemy actions several turns ahead. Synapse lets party members act during the planning phase and consumes Neura charges that build over turns. Sigils modify individual Neoran Actions, while Traits are passive modifiers, some with field-wide effects.
