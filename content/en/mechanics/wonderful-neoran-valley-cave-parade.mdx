## What the Roguelite Actually Uses: Timeline and Synapse

Wonderful Neoran Valley is a roguelite monster collector, and its confirmed combat hook is the Timeline. The Knight has foresight, so the Timeline shows enemy actions several turns ahead. That lets you plan offense and defense around known enemy patterns rather than reacting blindly.

Synapse extends the Knight’s time-altering ability to the party. It lets party members act during the planning phase and consumes Neura charges that build over turns. Every Neoran has its own Synapse action, according to the official gameplay description.

## Recruitment, Jewels, and Sigils
